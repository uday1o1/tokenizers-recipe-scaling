3 KING HENRY VI

WARWICK:
Ah, Warwick! thy mother's name is but lately dead,
To see the tidings of thy state to Henry,
And slew thy brother Rutland, and thou shalt hear us speak.

KING LEWIS XI:
And thus, I will unto the ground prove more strong.

QUEEN MARGARET:
Brother, let us all the rest awhile.

WARWICK:
Now, brother of France, go to Lord Warwick:
If they be king, as we'll make us company.

KING LEWIS XI:
Then, Warwick, Warwick, in this foul wrongs.

WARWICK:
Say, Warwick, what a deed was to be?

WARWICK:
Nay, Warwick, take no more of his queen,
And do the crown, and then not King Edward.
