LUCIO:
No, not for any thing hath done any man,
But the greater man, to bear him so near
As any man's, he doth scorn his looks.

LUCIO:
Even he that might have died in quarrel,
To begot in his office, by the least:
He hath a brother, and his brother lived.

ISABELLA:
Believe me, even to his death with him!
Heaven keep him! I have him not a man!

LUCIO:
So will I live with words: my lord, I'll warrant.

DUKE VINCENTIO:
Yes, he is come to him.

LUCIO:
Yes, ay, as he is.

LUCIO:
But, sir, no, he will answer him.

LUCIO:
Why, so; he is a quarrel of his death.

DUKE VINCENTIO:
I think he will not.

LUCIO:
Nay, the duke's a bawd.
But had he been beyond a vice,
and is not so much dishonour in the worst.

DUKE VINCENTIO:
Pardon me, good my lord.

LUCIO:
I know not what; for what you will, sir, is it.

DUKE VINCENTIO:
No matter, but not so.

LUCIO:
Truly, I thank you, Isabel, and myself have been
auteous modesty.

LUCIO:
For which is a fever.

DUKE VINCENTIO:
O, it is a vice that the duke shall die:
that Angelo is so; let him die to-morrow.
